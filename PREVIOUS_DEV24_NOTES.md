# RoosterWorks Ground Modules — v0.8.0-dev.24 TEST

Adds HPT-250, a compact 1.2 m-long equipment collar with nominal 2.5 m inline
interfaces and integrated flat hardpoints on both flanks. Each pad provides
a solid receiving collider and central structural snap node; surface mounting
works too. Both side faces remain within 2.5 m across. Supported roof rails,
ring/finish options, illuminated ends and continuous underside pipes match
the set. Both axial ends dock; side pads are structural mounts.

This collar is legless and belongs between supported modules. Fit external
loads clear of stairs/legs and balance them. DRILL can snap to a side node;
VT retains its original surface attachment and clean whole-pod release.

See COMPACT_HARDPOINT.md for dimensions, mounting, source and verification.
Existing sixteen ground parts and all plugin binaries remain unchanged,
including the dev.23 ISRU, drill rig and universal mounting shoe. Industry
instructions/rates remain in INDUSTRY.md.

Requires KSP 1.12.5, ModuleManager and Station Modules dev.5 or newer. No new
dependency. Close KSP, replace only GameData/RoosterWorksGroundModules and
restart. Keep Station Modules. Place new parts for testing; existing craft
and saves are not modified or migrated. Full in-game acceptance is pending.

Current builder: Tools/build_v24.py; validator: Tools/validate_v24.py.
Editable source: Source/Hardpoint/RW_GND_HPT.blend. Current previews use dev24
names. Complete install/source archives are in ../outputs/.
